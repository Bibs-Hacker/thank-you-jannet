04 — A letter

Dear Jannet.

You probably weren't expecting a website from me.

And honestly, I could have just written a normal message.

But sometimes ordinary words don't quite capture the appreciation behind them.

So I built this little universe instead.

I want you to know that I genuinely appreciate the friendship you have built with Mercy.

Campus is a completely different world. New people. New places. New experiences. New challenges.

And whenever you are together — laughing, talking, having fun or simply passing time — I know Mercy isn't experiencing that chapter completely alone.

That's something I value.

You don't have to do anything extraordinary to deserve appreciation. Sometimes simply being a good friend is extraordinary enough.

So thank you. For the laughter. For the company. For the friendship. For being part of Mercy's campus story.

Keep being yourself. Keep creating memories. Keep making the people around you smile.

And whenever you look back at your campus years, I hope there will be countless moments that make you smile.

This page is simply my way of saying: I see the good you bring.

Brian Through Mercy • With appreciation
